# Pilot scope — 2 weeks, marketing package for a startup that has a finished product

Status: draft for the owner to approve. Nothing here is agreed with any client yet.

## 1. Who this is for

A startup that already has a working product and nothing else: no positioning, no voice, no site copy, no content, no measurement. They can answer product questions and supply screenshots and access, and they name one person who approves.

## 2. In scope (the whole pilot, nothing else)

| # | Deliverable | Owner | Count | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Positioning brief | Shikamaru (`positioning-brief`) | 1 file | ICP, category, one line, message hierarchy, proof list with sources, ban list, `Unknown` section all filled; owner approved |
| 2 | Voice file from the client's real writing | Yugi | 1 file | Built from 3+ real samples the client supplied; use/ban lists concrete; owner approved |
| 3 | Landing page copy | Ronin | 1 file | Headline, sub, 3 proof blocks, objections, CTA, meta title/description; every claim traceable to the positioning proof list |
| 4 | Social posts | Ronin | 10 posts, 5 platforms (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok script, Twitter) | Native to each platform per `formats/`, no cross-posting of identical text, no unverified numbers |
| 5 | SEO articles | Lelouch → Quintaro → Edward | 2 articles | Brief, on-page pass, meta + schema package, target question stated per article |
| 6 | Design assets | Zoro | 3 banners + 1 email header | Rendered PNG at the stated sizes, opens, text from the approved copy, on-palette |
| 7 | Editor gate | Usop | 1 pass over items 3–6 | Written PASS/FAIL per item with reasons; 2 rounds maximum |
| 8 | Weekly readout format | Xaiyan | 1 template | Table the client fills with their real numbers, with Keep/Stop/Try columns defined |
| 9 | Delivery report | Tsunade | 1 file | Every item above listed with pass/fail and evidence path |

## 3. Out of scope (say it in writing, before signature)

- Brand identity: logo, identity system, brand book, trademark clearance, font licensing.
- Website build, hosting, CMS, analytics installation.
- Paid advertising of any kind.
- Publishing, scheduling, commenting, DMs: the team drafts, the client posts. No client credentials are taken.
- Guarantees of traffic, ranking, leads or revenue.
- Strategy or work for any market outside the one named in the scope.

## 4. Client must supply, by day 1

Product name, what it does, price and plans, 3 real screenshots, 3 competitor names, 3 samples of their own writing, one approver, and a review window. Missing inputs stop the clock; they do not become assumptions.

## 5. Review and rounds

One approver per side. Each review round answers within 2 business days. Two rounds per deliverable maximum; a third round is a new task and is quoted separately.

## 6. Price and payment

Set by the owner. The pilot is priced as a fixed fee for the deliverables in the table above, with hours tracked only to prove the margin. No success fee in the pilot: there is no baseline to measure against yet.

## 7. What this pilot is really measuring

Not the client's revenue. It measures: can this team take an outside brief, hit a date, pass an outside approver, and prove it with files? If it cannot, the answer is a smaller scope or no client, not more people.
